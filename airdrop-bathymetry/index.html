Model notes. Quadrotor: trapezoidal horizontal profile (accel-limited), hover power scaled with payload mass (P ∝ m^1.5), robots descend on a small drogue. Marine robot: unicycle with first-order motor/yaw lag, bounded turn rate, ocean-current drift, OU yaw/speed noise; A* planning on obstacle-inflated grid, pure-pursuit tracking, next waypoint becomes active when the current one is reached. Footprint radius r = depth · tan(β/2) (β = full beam angle). Sensor samples depth at the robot position at the chosen rate (+ Gaussian noise). GP: squared-exponential kernel, fixed hyper-parameters, incremental Cholesky; samples closer than ℓ/20 to an already-used one are skipped to bound cost. Robots do not avoid one another. Bathymetry is synthetic (seeded).
